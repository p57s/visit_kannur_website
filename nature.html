Serene Beaches

Kannur is home to Asia's longest drive-in beach, Muzhappilangad beach and, in contrast, you can find more secluded and calm beaches such as Kizhunna, Thottada beach. Spend a playful day in the sun-heated sand and the graceful waves. Lastly, stop at Payyambalam beach to enjoy a long walk overlooking the magnificent sea.

Relaxing Nature

This district has extremely geographically varying locations with high altitude mountains to mangrove backwaters and numerous beaches. An underrated place to just relax and take in the beauty of nature is Kunnathurpadi, featuring the parts of the Sahyadri Mountains. To experience a more tropical area of Kannur, visit Kavvayi backwaters, where you can kayak your way through the mangroves and also watch the sea meet the creek. There are also boat rides to the islands where, if you are lucky, you can get a beach all to yourself. During dry season, you can also walk amongst the greenery as the water level depletes, or stay at the Coconut Island camping site, which offers a good time with informative guides at a reasonable charge.

Breathtaking Views

Palakkayam Thattu is lovely hill station located 3500ft above the sea level with a panoramic view of greenery. Witness its scenic beauty by trekking up to the peak. There are also many adventurous activities, such as zip lining, rock climbing and other rope activities. If hiking is not feasible, you can opt for a jumpy jeep ride to the top. The best time to visit this site is during the months of October-February and, if you wish, you can also camp at the location.
