Culture

Temples

You can find numerous temples in this region having beautifully built structures housing the deities, such as the Peralassery temple in Mundalur has a massive ancient step well that is a sight to see.

Theyyam

This is a vibrant, ancient ritual performed by people from the Malayan and Vannan communities, who are believed to be possessed by a deity. Some of the greats are Theechamundi, Gulikan, Sree Muthappan and Pottan Theyyam.

Historical sites

Kannur is a city that is historically rich with sites such as St. Angelo's fort and Arakkal museum, which signifies the growth of this wonderful city. The lighthouse at Payyambalam beach is also a must-visit with a view enclosing the whole township.
