Cuisine

Fresh from the sea

For a seafood enthusiast, Kannur offers one of the best varieties of locally sourced seafood. To get a first-hand experience of hustling for fresh fish, visit the Ayikkara Fishing Harbour in Burnacherry.

Traditional Delicacies

Explore the local cuisine. There are several dishes that make use of the regionally available ingredients. Some of the most loved delicacies are pathiris with chicken curry and kappa with fish curry.

Sadya

One of the most satisfying meals offered by this cuisine includes several dishes served on a banana leaf, starting from the simple banana chips to the pulissery.
